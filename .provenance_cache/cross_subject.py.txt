CAVEAT on that gap, and it is not a small one:
  - The within-subject column is read from results/sweep_results.csv, which sweep_subjects.py
    produced with StratifiedKFold per subject on that subject's own trials.
    The cross-subject column is LeaveOneGroupOut over pooled trials. Two
    estimators, two fold structures -- so this is NOT a paired comparison on
    identical folds, and the difference is not purely a transfer cost.
  - sweep_subjects.py documents that its epoch tail runs off the end of some
    recordings, so MNE silently drops trials and those subjects' accuracies
    land off the k/n lattice. Nothing here checks whether any subject in this
    subset is one of them.
