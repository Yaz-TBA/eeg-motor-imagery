6. ARM (a), UNIVARIATE: DOES LOG HIGH-BAND POWER DIFFER BY CLASS?
==============================================================================

Per trial per channel: mean band power over the feature window, then log.
Log because raw band power is right-skewed and heteroscedastic in a way
that scales with the class mean, which is the same argument this repo
already makes for log=True inside CSP.

Two-sided, because there is no defensible directional prior. The cue is a
bar at the TOP of the screen for fists and the BOTTOM for feet, which gives
a class-dependent gaze direction but no prediction about which direction
produces more high-band temporal power.

BOTH tests must agree for a positive call. Disagreement is reported as
outlier-trial-driven, which is itself the realistic EMG failure mode: a few
trials with a clench, not a shifted distribution.

PRE-COMPUTED POWER, so a null cannot be oversold. With 21 vs 24 trials at 80% power:
  minimum detectable Cohen's d = 0.837 for the aggregate test at alpha 0.05
  minimum detectable Cohen's d = 1.069 for a single channel at alpha 0.00625
Both are LARGE effects. A null on arm (a) bounds large effects only.

8. THE SENSITIVITY LADDER: WHAT SIZE OF PLANTED SOURCE CAN THIS PROBE SEE?
==============================================================================

This is the part that separates measuring from conceding. Without it, a
probe at floor supports only 'we looked and found nothing'. With it, the
probe supports 'we can detect a class-correlated broadband temporal source
of size X, and this recording contains less than X'.

DESIGN. A realistic muscle artifact is a SOURCE: one generator projecting
to several electrodes with a fixed topography. Independent per-channel noise
was rejected as unrealistic and as unfair to CSP, whose entire business is
finding coherent spatial directions.

  per trial, one latent Gaussian series, band-limited with the SAME filter
  cascade as the primary band, projected onto the 8 temporal channels with a
  fixed unit-norm topography.
  amplitude a = the source's contribution to T8 as a fraction of T8's own
  measured high-band SD, so the ladder is in interpretable units.
  10 injection seeds per rung, distinct from the CV seed, which stays 42.
  BOTH directions (into the 21 hands, and separately into the 24 feet). The
  WORSE, meaning higher, detection threshold is the one reported as the
  bound. A bound computed from the easier direction overstates the instrument.
  detection = smallest a whose MEDIAN accuracy across 10 seeds reaches 31/45 = 68.9%.
  31/45 rather than 30/45 because 30/45 sits at binomial p = 0.0490 and a detection
  criterion should not rest on a knife edge.

THE TOPOGRAPHY IS STIPULATED, NOT MEASURED. It is a plausible right
temporalis shape, not this subject's. That is why a spatially flat

8B. POST-REGISTRATION LADDER EXTENSION (added 2026-07-26, NOT BLIND)
==============================================================================
Everything in this section was added after the registered ladder had run and
after an adversarial pass reported what it would find. It is disclosed as an
addition, the pre-registration is not edited to accommodate it, and section
8's registered numbers above are left exactly as they printed.

WHY a IS NOT A SHAPE-FREE UNIT. a is defined as the source's contribution
to T8 as a fraction of T8's own high-band SD. Detectability depends on the
TOTAL power the source puts on the ring. Those two are related by the
topography: with w the unit-norm shape, the injected amplitude is
a * SD_T8 / w[T8], so total injected power scales as 1 / w[T8]^2. A flat
source at a given a therefore injects far more total power than a focal one
at the same a, and is correspondingly easier for the probe to see. The
registered 'max over topographies' is a max over TWO shapes, both of them on
the diffuse side.

--- The limitations that survive every possible outcome ---
  1. SPECTRAL SCOPE, corrected 2026-07-26. This limitation used to read
     'even a perfect null bounds only the RECORDED part of the spectrum',
     which names the wrong boundary in the direction that overstates
     coverage: the recorded spectrum is 0 to 80 Hz and this probe covers
     40 to 75 Hz minus 56 to 64 Hz. Two truncations, not one:
     160 Hz sampling discards everything above the 80 Hz Nyquist, where surface
     temporalis EMG has substantial power, AND this probe declines the
     40 Hz below its own lower edge. Nothing at all is bounded inside the
     decoder's own 8 to 30 Hz passband, which is the only band the headline
     can be contaminated in.
  2. The average reference is computed over all 64 channels BEFORE any
     subset is picked, so every channel carries -1/64 of every other and the
     temporal ring is NOT electrically sealed off. This BOUNDS a
     contribution; it does not isolate one.
  3. EEGMMIDB ships NO EOG and NO EMG reference channel. There is no ground
     truth for 'this is muscle'. This probe measures high-band power at
     muscle-adjacent scalp sites. It does not measure muscle.
  4. A positive cannot distinguish temporalis EMG from a saccadic spike
     potential, and the position-confounded cue makes the latter plausible.
  5. n = 45. Every accuracy here has a Wilson interval well over 20 points
     wide, and a four-trial difference between conditions is noise.
  6. Single subject, single session. Runs 6, 10 and 14 are three recordings
     from ONE session, and a session-level trend across all three survives
     every control in this design.
  7. A null does NOT license 'EMG is not a risk for this project'. Imagined
     fists versus feet is a low-EMG task. Imagined SPEECH, the corpus's own
     worked example, is a task where sub-vocalisation makes EMG
     class-correlated by construction.
  8. The injection topography is STIPULATED, not measured. Section 8B now
     measures how much that costs: over four shapes the threshold spans a
     multiple of itself in the registered units, so the registered figure is
     not a bound over topographies.

     leakage, but not inside the fold either.
